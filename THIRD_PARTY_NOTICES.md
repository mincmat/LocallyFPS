# Third-party runtime components

Official LocallyFPS release packages include the following unmodified runtime components:

- FFmpeg, distributed under the license included beside its binaries. Linux and Windows builds come from [BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds](https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds); the Apple Silicon build comes from [eugeneware/ffmpeg-static](https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static).
- `rife-ncnn-vulkan` and the RIFE v4.6 model from [nihui/rife-ncnn-vulkan](https://github.com/nihui/rife-ncnn-vulkan), distributed under its included MIT license.

Exact upstream versions, URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `packaging/runtime_manifest.json`. LocallyFPS itself remains licensed under the MIT license in `LICENSE`.
